New AI standard combines practical automation, human oversight and governance across Aareon's technology portfolio Aareon UK has launched Aareon Intelligence, a new approach to artificial intelligence designed specifically for the property sector. Aareon Intelligence brings together the AI capabilities used across Aareon's software into a single framework for governance, testing, security, and oversight. It helps property organisations deal with growing workloads, increasing regulation and rising customer expectations, without creating more work for their teams.
