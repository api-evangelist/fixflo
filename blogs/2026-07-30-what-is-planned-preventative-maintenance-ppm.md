Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM) is a proactive approach to property maintenance that involves scheduling inspections, servicing and repairs before issues arise. Rather than waiting for something to break, PPM helps property managers reduce reactive maintenance, stay compliant with legal requirements and extend the lifespan of their buildings and assets.
