Contractors using Simpro can now receive, manage and complete property maintenance work more efficiently through a connected workflow that reduces administration, removes duplicate data entry and keeps clients informed automatically. This partnership brings together two specialist platforms that serve different parts of the maintenance process. Fixflo is where property managers receive, triage, instruct, and oversee repairs.
