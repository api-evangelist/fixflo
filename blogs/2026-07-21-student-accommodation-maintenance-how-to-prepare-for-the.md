Every year, student accommodation operators brace themselves for the same challenge: the September surge. Thousands of students arrive pretty much all at once during arrivals week, bringing energy, excitement… and a tidal wave of maintenance requests. From broken appliances to mystery leaks, blocked drains, lost user manuals, and the classic “ my radiator isn’t working ” report, the first few weeks can stretch even the most experienced facilities teams.
